## Project Overview

This Oakland family wanted to modernize their outdated 1980s kitchen while maintaining the home's character. The result is a stunning transformation that blends contemporary style with timeless appeal.

### Design Highlights

The new kitchen features warm wood tones paired with crisp white quartz countertops. The custom cabinets maximize storage while maintaining an open, airy feel. Strategic lighting creates ambiance while providing excellent task lighting for food preparation.
