## Creating a Spa Experience

This master bathroom renovation transformed a cramped, dated space into a luxurious spa-like retreat. The clients wanted a serene, modern aesthetic with high-end finishes.

### Key Features

The star of the show is the spacious walk-in shower with frameless glass enclosure and a stunning rainfall showerhead. Large format tiles create a seamless, contemporary look while heated floors add a touch of luxury.

### Materials Used

- 24x48 porcelain tile flooring
- Marble-look wall tile
- Quartz vanity countertop
- Custom floating vanity
- Premium fixtures throughout

### Timeline

This project was completed in just 3 weeks, minimizing disruption to the homeowners' daily routine.
